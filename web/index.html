Remote accessOpen Cadet from your phone or any browser, behind a password. Nothing is reachable until you turn it on.

This needs Cloudflare's free cloudflared. Install it with brew install cloudflared, then reopen this window.

The domain must be on the Cloudflare account you sign in with. Cadet creates the tunnel and the DNS record for you.

Zero trust: require Cloudflare Access

Cloudflare checks who is visiting (email code, Google, GitHub…) before anything reaches Cadet. Cadet verifies that check on every request, on top of the password. In Cloudflare Zero Trust, add a Self-hosted application for your domain with a policy that allows only you, then paste its details here. Needs your own domain.

A quick link needs no Cloudflare account, but changes every time you turn it on. Use your own domain for an address that stays the same.
